06 / Frequently asked

Before we get started.

How long does a project take?

A typical project takes around 2–3 days. Final timing depends on video length, complexity, footage quality and the agreed scope. I’ll confirm a timeline before starting.

How much do you charge?

Pricing depends on the project, length, editing style, deliverables and deadline. Share your brief and reference, and I’ll provide a quote before work begins.

What do you need from me?

Usually the raw footage, a short brief, reference links, target platform, aspect ratio, any brand assets and the deadline.

Are revisions included?

Revision rounds should be agreed in the quote before the project starts. Major changes to the original brief may affect the price and delivery date.

Which formats can you deliver?

We’ll agree on the required platform and aspect ratio in advance, such as 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for Reels and Shorts.

Can you edit brand ads and UGC?

Yes — I offer brand ads and UGC-style Reels. Share the product, target audience, key message and any required deliverables.
